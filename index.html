Research Gap Finder

Discover evidence-backed candidate research gaps from several recent journal literature.

Definition of "candidate gap"

This agent does not determine whether a topic has never been studied. Instead, it identifies signals within the retrieved literature sample that may indicate areas worth further investigation.

Candidate Gap Signals

How the agent decides what counts as a candidate gap: it looks for three types of evidence patterns in the retrieved literature sample.

01

Method Coverage Gap

A research method that is rarely represented within a particular context in the retrieved literature sample.

Exploratory coverage signal based on method × context representation.
02

Recurring Limitation

An explicitly stated limitation or future-work need that appears repeatedly across retrieved paper abstracts.

Grounded in limitations explicitly stated by paper authors.
03

Conflicting Findings

Studies within a similar research setting that report different directions of findings.

May indicate an unresolved or context-dependent relationship.
